Slum to an Entrepreneur – Founder Journey

Every great journey begins with a dream. Aditya Kuttan grew up in a slum in Colaba, Mumbai with limited resources but unlimited dreams. Starting as a Housekeeping Associate at Café Coffee Day, he worked his way up over 20 years in hospitality across India, the UAE, Oman, and Qatar to become Head of Operations.

Three years ago, he founded Mom's Cloud Kitchen from Virar, Maharashtra, delivering over 4,000+ orders with authentic home-style flavors and pure-veg quality.

"I started my career cleaning floors in a coffee shop. Today, I dream of building a food brand that reaches thousands of homes. The journey is proof that your background doesn't decide your future — your determination does." — Aditya Kuttan, Founder
